title: Team invitations
description: Invite somebody to a Team or open an invitation while preserving its exact Home and access intent.

Use a Team invitation to bring somebody into one Team on one Home, with the role
and Session-history choice selected by a Team manager.

<Callout>
  **Developer Preview:** native Team invitations and their email delivery are
  implemented in Happier's development source. They are not released or
  publicly available yet. Real SMTP delivery, CID-QR scanning, browser refresh,
  and native deep-link journeys remain unverified from the current source.
</Callout>

## What you need first

- To send an invitation, you need permission to manage that Team.
- For invitation email, the Home must have working email delivery. A
  transferable link remains available when email delivery is not configured.
- Keep the complete invitation URL together. It carries the one-time bearer and
  a non-secret description of the exact Home that may receive it.

## Send an invitation

Open **Settings → Teams**, choose the Team, then open **Invitations** and create
a new invitation.

Choose a role and, when the Team permits it, whether existing Team Session
history is included. Then choose one delivery:

- **Invite by link** creates a transferable link. Copy or share it only with the
  intended person.
- **Invite by email** binds admission to the named mailbox and sends the link
  from the Home. It is shown only when the Home reports that email delivery is
  ready.

The full link is revealed only when it is issued or reissued. The invitation
list keeps status and masked delivery details, not the bearer. Reissuing retires
the old link before presenting a replacement; revoking makes an outstanding
link unusable.

## Accept an invitation

Open the complete link in Happier. The first screen identifies the Team and its
Home before asking you to authenticate.

- If you already use that Home, sign in with one of the methods it offers.
- If you need a new Account, a current invitation can expose only the account
  mode and sign-in action that its Home permits for this admission.
- If an email-bound invitation names another address, Happier lets you switch
  Account or explicitly continue with the current Account and prove control of
  the invited mailbox. It never merges Accounts silently.

After authentication, Happier rechecks the invitation and Team policy, accepts
membership once, and continues to the intended Team. Signing in to the Home by
itself does not prove Team membership.

## When it goes wrong

- An invalid, expired, revoked, or already-used link cannot admit an Account.
  Ask a Team manager to reissue it when appropriate.
- If Happier cannot identify the Home from the link, nothing is sent to a
  guessed server. Ask for the complete link again.
- If this device has not added the named Home, add that Home and reopen the
  invitation afterwards.
- If email delivery fails, the manager can retry delivery or reissue. An
  email-bound invitation does not silently become a transferable link.
- If Team authentication policy changes while you sign in, Happier preserves
  valid Home authentication and asks for a currently allowed alternative. It
  does not sign you out of unrelated personal work.
