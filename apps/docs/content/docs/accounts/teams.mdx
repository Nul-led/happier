title: Teams
description: Create a Team on one Home, give people roles, group them, and decide what new sessions do by default.
---

A Team is one shared place on one Home for sessions, people, and access. Create
one and you can share a session with everybody in it instead of naming people one
at a time, hand out shared credentials once, and set what new sessions do by
default for the whole group.

## Availability

Teams are a per-Home capability, offered unless the Home's operator turns them
off — on a self-hosted Home, with `HAPPIER_FEATURE_TEAMS__ENABLED`. A client that
reaches a Home without Teams shows **Teams are not available on this Home**
rather than half a feature, and everything else on that Home keeps working.

A Team belongs to the Home it was created on and stays there. If you use more
than one Home, each one has its own Teams, and the Teams list tells you which
Home each Team came from. When a Home cannot be reached, its Teams are missing
from the list and Happier says so instead of pretending the list is complete.

## Create a Team

Open **Settings → Teams** and choose **New Team**. Give it a **Name** and, if it
helps, a **Description**. The Team is created on the Home you are looking at, and
you become its first owner.

Two Teams may share a name. Names are labels: invitation links and access always
refer to the Team itself, never to its name.

Some Homes administer Team creation centrally. There you see **Team creation is
administered on this Home** and an administrator creates the Team and chooses its
**First owner**. Creating a Team for somebody else does not add you to it.

## Roles

Every membership carries exactly one role, and the role decides what the person
gets by default:

| Role | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Owns the Team, may manage it, and may change owners. |
| **Admin** | Receives Member access and may manage the Team. |
| **Member** | Receives access granted to the Team by default. |
| **Guest** | Sees only Sessions and resources explicitly shared with this Account or with a Group it belongs to. |

Guest is the role to reach for when somebody should collaborate on a few named
sessions without seeing the Team's shared work. The invitation screen says so
before they accept: joining as Guest does not give access to that Team's Team
Sessions.

A Team always keeps at least one active owner. If you try to remove, suspend, or
demote the last one, Happier stops you with **A Team keeps at least one active
owner** and asks you to choose another owner first.

## Members

The **Members** tab lists everybody in the Team with their role and status, and
filters for owners and admins, members, guests, and suspended people. **Add
member** adds somebody who already has an Account on this Home; to reach anybody
else, send an [invitation](/accounts/team-invitations).

Three lifecycle actions, each with a different blast radius:

- **Suspend member** stops Team and Group access immediately while keeping the
  person's Group membership and resource assignments. Reactivating restores only
  the access that is still valid. Their Home Account and their other Teams are
  untouched.
- **Reactivate member** resumes access where memberships, Groups, and Account
  state still allow it.
- **Remove from Team** ends current Team and Group access and removes Group
  memberships and membership-lifetime grants. It does not erase prior authorship
  or content the person already saw. Rejoining later starts a new membership.

Where a membership comes from a directory, the row says **Managed through** that
source and the role is read-only here — change it in the source. You can move a
membership between Happier and a directory under **Management source**; that
keeps the membership, its role, status, and session history, and changes only who
may edit it.

### Encrypted access, and what Prepare does

If your Home stores sessions with end-to-end encryption, a new member's Account
cannot read existing Team session history until somebody who can read it prepares
that access for them. The member's detail screen shows this under **Encrypted
access**:

- **Ready** — nothing to do. They can open the Team's encrypted sessions.
- **Needs preparing** — the sessions exist, the member cannot read them yet.
  Choose **Prepare encrypted access** and Happier re-wraps each session's key for
  that member's Account from your device, showing progress as it goes.
- **Setup required** — the person has not finished setting up encrypted access on
  their own Account. Prepare becomes available once they have.
- **Not encrypted** — their Account does not use end-to-end encryption, so there
  is nothing to prepare.

Preparing runs from your device, using your own access, so it can only cover
sessions you can currently read. If some cannot be prepared from this device you
see **Encrypted access needs repair**: open those sessions once to repair your own
access, then prepare again. Sessions created in an older encryption format cannot
be shared with new members at all; they stay readable for the people who already
have them. If preparation stops partway, whatever was already prepared is kept —
run it again to finish.

## Groups

A Group is a flat set of Team members you can share with together: **Developers**,
**Design**, **On-call**. Create one from the **Groups** tab, add members, and from
then on you can grant a session or a credential to the Group instead of to each
person.

Groups do not nest and do not carry roles of their own. A person's role comes from
their Team membership; a Group only decides what gets shared with them as a set.

Archiving a Group stops Group-based access immediately while keeping its
membership and history, so restoring it can make those grants effective again. A
Group fed by a directory shows **Managed by** that source: its name and lifecycle
belong to the source, though you can still add people to it in Happier.

## What new sessions do by default

The **Settings** tab holds the decisions that apply to the whole Team.

**Session defaults** decides what happens to a session somebody starts in this
Team:

- **Private by default** — nothing is shared until the person shares it.
- **Shared with the Team by default** — new sessions start visible to the Team,
  and the person can still make one private.
- **Always shared with the Team** — new sessions are shared and stay that way.

This applies to new sessions only. Existing private sessions are never exposed by
changing it.

**External sharing** decides who may share a Team session outside the Team —
**Anyone who can share**, **Team admins only**, or **Not allowed**. It blocks
future sharing; it cannot withdraw a copy already shared.

**History default** preselects the **Session history** choice offered when
somebody joins: **Include sessions already shared with the Team**, or **Only
sessions shared after they join**. It is a default for new members, not a rewrite
of the history existing members already have. The choice works at whole-session
granularity — it does not reveal only the messages created after somebody joined.

A **Logo** replaces the Team's monogram everywhere the Team appears. Team and
Group names, descriptions, Group membership, and policy are administration data
the Home can read, including on an end-to-end encrypted Account, so keep secrets
out of them.

## Archiving a Team

**Archive** moves a Team out of active views. Team- and Group-based access stops
and outstanding invitation links are revoked, while memberships, Groups, policies,
and existing grants are retained — restoring the Team may make those retained
grants effective again. Revoked invitation links do not come back; issue new ones.
Archived Teams stay listed under **Show archived** and are read-only until
restored.

## Team sign-in

A Team can require a particular sign-in — your company IdP rather than an email
and password — and can receive its membership from a directory instead of from
invitations. That lives on the **Authentication** tab and is set up together with
the Home operator; see [Enterprise
identity](/self-hosting/auth-enterprise-identity) for the whole model, including
the Team's shareable member sign-in page, WorkOS, GitHub organizations, and
directory sync.

Signing in to a Home never joins a Team by itself. Admission is a separate
decision the Team's policy makes.

## When it goes wrong

| What you see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Teams are not available on this Home** | The Home has Teams turned off, needs an update, or is unreachable. |
| **Showing the last known data for this Home** | You are looking at cached state because the Home is not reachable right now. |
| **Someone else changed this first** | Another manager saved first. Review the current values, then apply your change again. |
| **You do not have permission for this change** | Your role does not allow it. Owners and admins manage the Team. |
| **This Team is archived** | Restore it before making changes. |
| **The Home may have completed this change** | The result was lost in transit. Refresh the Team before retrying, rather than repeating the change blind. |
