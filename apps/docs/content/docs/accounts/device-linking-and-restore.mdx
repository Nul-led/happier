Happier now has a few different QR-based flows. They look similar, but they solve different problems.

> **Availability:** Direct Home pairing and the `happier home pair-device` / `happier home link-account` commands are available in the 0.3 development/preview line. Stable 0.2 keeps its released device-linking flows.

This page explains which one to use, what each button means, and what to do when QR scanning or server reachability gets in the way.

## The three things users commonly want

Most account/device questions fall into one of these buckets:

- **Sign in on a new phone** using a computer that is already signed in
- **Restore access on a new computer or browser** using a saved secret key
- **Connect a terminal** to your account so the CLI/daemon can run sessions

These are separate flows on purpose:

- **Account restore / add a phone** signs a new device into the same Happier account
- **Terminal connect** authorizes a terminal/daemon, not a new user-facing device

If you remember that distinction, the rest of the UI is much easier to reason about.

## What each option means

### Add your phone

Use **Settings → Add your phone** on desktop/web when:

- you are already signed in on your computer
- you want your phone to sign in to that same Happier account

That screen shows:

- a QR code
- a temporary link behind **Show link**, for devices that cannot scan
- the Home and expiry for the invite

The deliberate scan is the authorization for direct Home QR linking. Happier binds the request to
the QR, the exact Home, the joining device's temporary public key, and the invite expiry, then the
signed-in computer completes the first valid request automatically. There is no comparison code or
second approval step in this direct QR flow.

The live QR and temporary link are short-lived bearer capabilities. Keep them private: anyone with
the complete live QR or link could race your device before the invite expires. If it is exposed,
do not use it; let it expire and create a new QR.

### Restore account

Use **Restore account** on a logged-out device when you want that device to join an existing account.

What happens next depends on the device and whether Happier already knows the exact Home:

- **Phone / mobile-sized web with camera support:** Happier opens the QR scanner first so you can scan a desktop/web QR immediately
- **Desktop / larger web layouts with a verified saved Home:** the signed-out desktop can display a temporary QR for a device already enrolled in that same Home to scan
- **A completely new desktop that does not know the Home:** use the secret-key recovery path, or enter a temporary link from the signed-in Home when that input is available

The requester-displayed QR is deliberately limited to a Home profile Happier already saved and can
verify. The enrolled phone resolves that exact Home, starts the request there, and returns a
credential sealed for the requesting desktop. A fresh desktop cannot discover an unknown Home by
displaying its own static QR; it needs the secret key, a temporary Home link, or another supported
discovery path first.

### Login with secret key instead

Use **Login with secret key instead** when:

- you do not have another signed-in device available
- scanning is not possible
- you intentionally keep a manual backup / recovery path

For device-key accounts, the secret key is the recovery credential that lets a new device read the same encrypted account data.

### Scan QR to connect terminal

Use **Scan QR to connect terminal** only when a terminal or CLI is showing a Happier terminal QR.

This does **not** sign a phone or browser into your account. It approves a terminal/daemon connection.

### Enter URL manually

Use **Enter URL manually** when:

- the camera is unavailable
- the QR code is on another screen and you cannot scan it directly
- you copied a Happier link from another device

This is the manual fallback for QR-driven flows. It is especially useful on locked-down browsers, remote devices, or when camera permissions are unreliable.

## Common user flows

### Add a phone from a signed-in computer

1. On the signed-in desktop/web app, open **Settings → Add your phone**
2. On the phone, choose **Restore account**
3. Scan the QR code from the computer
4. Keep both devices open while Happier verifies the request and adds the Home

After verification, the phone adds that Home. No comparison code or direct-QR approval is required.

The QR invite identifies one specific Home and carries that Home's connection details. Every start,
request, wait, and completion call goes to that Home; Happier does not temporarily switch to it or
reuse whichever Home happens to be focused. The new credential is saved under the invited Home, and
adding it leaves your current Home, sidebar groups, and other saved credentials unchanged.

From the Home host, `happier home pair-device` starts the same kind of new short-lived, Home-bound pairing session. Each retry creates fresh invitation material; do not reuse an expired or exposed QR/link. `happier home link-account` separately links the Home to your selected Account Service for discovery without transferring Home authority to that service.

To undo that link, use **Settings → Account → Homes → Stop delegated sign-in to this Home**, or run `happier home unlink-account [--home PROFILE]` on the Home host. Unlinking makes the Home refuse future delegated sign-ins from that service. It does **not** revoke Home credentials the service already issued: devices that are already signed in keep their access until you sign them out on the Home, and the Home stays listed in that service's Homes list.

### Restore a new browser or desktop

1. On the new browser/computer, open **Restore account**
2. If Happier shows a saved, verified Home, keep the requester QR open and scan it from a device already enrolled in that Home
3. Otherwise, choose **Login with secret key instead** and enter the recovery key you previously saved

If you are using a phone as the new device, Happier usually opens the supported direct Home QR
scanner first instead.

The requester-displayed QR uses the same short lifetime, Home identity binding, requester-key
binding, sealed credential response, and non-focusing adoption as the phone-scans-computer flow.
It does not add a comparison code or another approval prompt.

### QR codes from older Happier versions

> **Development status:** This compatibility behavior is implemented in current development
> builds and has not shipped in a supported release yet.

Current development builds recognize released QR V1 invitations only to explain how to recover.
They show: “This QR was created by an older version of Happier. Update Happier on the other device
and create a new QR.” You can then choose **Scan a new QR** or **Cancel**.

Recognition does not start or poll enrollment, transfer or save credentials, restore the old
six-digit comparison or direct **Approve**/**Reject** flow, convert the invitation to V2, or retain
or log its secret. Malformed or unknown QR data remains a separate invalid or unsupported result;
it does not receive the older-QR explanation.

Current development builds create only V2 invitations. V2 linking remains automatic and
code-free: update the device that created the old QR, then create and scan a new one.

### Connect a terminal

1. Start terminal login from the CLI
2. In the Happier app, choose **Scan QR to connect terminal**
3. Scan the terminal QR or paste the link with **Enter URL manually**
4. Approve the request

This gives the terminal access it needs without changing which devices are signed in to your account UI.

## QR safety and optional Home approval

Direct Home QR linking uses possession of the deliberately displayed, short-lived QR as its
authorization. The QR does not contain the final Home credential. Happier cryptographically binds
the request to the invited Home and joining device, and the Home returns the credential sealed for
that device after the signed-in client verifies the request.

For direct QR linking:

- start from the intended Home's **Add your phone** screen
- keep the live QR and temporary link private
- let an exposed or unexpected invite expire, then create a new QR
- check the Home name shown by Happier before continuing

Existing-device approval is a separate, optional Home policy for enrollment that starts through an
Account Service. When that policy is enabled, a fully signed-in device on the same Home receives one
explicit **Approve** or **Reject** decision. A directory/Account Service login, API token, terminal
credential, or assertion cannot make that decision or mint the Home credential.

Direct Home QR linking continues to work without an Account Service. If you discover a Home through
an Account Service instead, that service supplies only a short-lived assertion; the selected Home
still owns redemption, any required existing-device approval, and the final Home credential.

## Personal Homes, Iroh, and localhost

This is the main source of confusion in local development and some self-hosted setups.

If your server is configured as `localhost`, `127.0.0.1`, `::1`, or another loopback-only host:

- desktop/web can still talk to it locally
- a phone cannot reach that same address, because `localhost` on the phone means the phone itself

Current Home-device QR invites are strict and explicitly name the target Home and its reachable
connection descriptor. Happier does not remove that target and fall back to the currently active
Home. A Personal Home can publish an Iroh-only descriptor even while its HTTP listener remains on
loopback, so a public HTTPS URL is not required for device pairing.

If the Home has neither a reachable Iroh endpoint nor a reachable HTTPS endpoint, repair its
published descriptor and create a new QR invite. Retry stays bound to the same stable Home identity.

Working choices include:

- the Personal Home's published Iroh endpoint
- a LAN URL such as `http://192.168.x.x:53288` (native clients; browsers generally need HTTPS)
- a Tailscale HTTPS URL such as `https://machine.tailnet.ts.net`
- your own public/reverse-proxied domain

For advanced/operator HTTPS ingress, set `HAPPIER_PUBLIC_SERVER_URL` to an endpoint the new device can reach. `HAPPIER_CANONICAL_SERVER_URL` is compatibility identity metadata and may be different; current strict Home identity comes from the Home descriptor rather than whichever route happens to work.

## Which flow should I pick?

Use this quick rule:

- **I want my phone to sign in to my existing account** → **Add your phone**
- **I want a saved, verified Home to add this browser/computer** → **Restore account**, then scan its QR from a device already enrolled in that Home
- **This device does not know my Home yet** → use **Login with secret key instead** or a temporary link from that Home
- **I want the CLI/terminal to connect** → **Scan QR to connect terminal**
- **I cannot scan right now** → **Enter URL manually** or **Login with secret key instead**
